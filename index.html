Emberdrift — Privacy Policy

Emberdrift（余烬星海）隐私政策

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Emberdrift is a single-player card game. Your gameplay and progress data stay on your device. To meet platform requirements for game-category apps, the app integrates Huawei Game Service for account sign-in and real-name/minors-protection compliance checks; those checks are operated by Huawei. The developer operates no servers and does not receive or store any personal data.

Emberdrift 是一款单机卡牌游戏。您的游戏与进度数据仅保存在设备本地。为满足平台对游戏类应用的要求，本应用接入华为游戏服务（Huawei Game Service）以完成账号登录与实名/未成年人保护合规校验；该校验由华为运营。开发者不运营任何服务器，不接收、不存储任何个人数据。

1. Huawei Game Service (sign-in and compliance)

1. 华为游戏服务（登录与合规）

When you launch the app on a device that supports Huawei Game Service, the app calls Huawei's game service to sign in with your HUAWEI ID and to perform the real-name verification and minors' play-time checks required for game apps. In this step:

在支持华为游戏服务的设备上启动应用时，应用会调用华为游戏服务，使用您的华为账号登录，并执行游戏应用所要求的实名认证与未成年人游玩时长校验。在此过程中：

  • Huawei processes your HUAWEI ID account identifier, the account name shown on the sign-in panel, and the verification results (real-name status, age group, play-time status). This processing is governed by Huawei's own privacy policy.
  • The app receives only a game-player identifier and the sign-in/compliance result. It uses them solely to open the game and, when you switch game accounts, to clear the previous account's local progress.
  • The developer does not receive, collect, or store your account information, real-name status, or any identifiers — the app communicates with Huawei's service directly from your device, and the developer has no server.
  • 华为将处理您的华为账号标识、登录面板上展示的账号名称，以及校验结果（实名状态、年龄段、游玩时长状态）。该处理适用华为自身的隐私政策。
  • 应用仅收到游戏玩家标识与登录/合规结果，仅用于进入游戏，以及您切换游戏账号时清除前一账号的本地进度。
  • 开发者不接收、不收集、不存储您的账号信息、实名状态或任何标识——应用从您的设备直接与华为服务通信，开发者没有服务器。

2. Local Storage

2. 本地存储

Your game progress and settings are stored only in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. When you switch game accounts, local progress is cleared so accounts stay separate.

游戏进度与设置仅保存在您的设备本地。该数据永不离开您的设备，卸载应用即被删除。切换游戏账号时，本地进度会被清除以保证账号间数据隔离。

3. Third Parties, Ads and Analytics

3. 第三方、广告与统计

The app contains no advertising and no analytics SDKs. The only third-party service used is Huawei Game Service, as described in section 1.

游戏不含广告、无分析统计 SDK。唯一使用的第三方服务为华为游戏服务（见第 1 节）。

4. Network

4. 网络

The app requests the network permission (INTERNET) solely to reach Huawei Game Service for the sign-in/compliance step. Gameplay itself runs offline. If the service is unavailable (for example, no network connection), the game remains playable in offline mode.

应用申请网络权限（INTERNET）仅用于访问华为游戏服务完成上述登录/合规步骤。游戏玩法本身离线运行。当该服务不可用（例如无网络连接）时，游戏仍可以离线模式游玩。

5. Children's Privacy

5. 儿童隐私

Real-name verification and minors' play-time protection are performed by Huawei in accordance with applicable regulations. The developer collects no data from anyone, including children.

实名认证与未成年人游玩时长保护由华为按照适用法规执行。开发者不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

6. Permissions

6. 权限

  • ohos.permission.INTERNET — used only for the Huawei Game Service sign-in/compliance calls described above.
  • ohos.permission.INTERNET——仅用于上述华为游戏服务登录/合规调用。
